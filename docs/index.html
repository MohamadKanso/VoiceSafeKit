Local voice safety layer v0.5.0

Catch the private details speech-to-text writes as words.

Transcripts say “four two four two…” and “alex dot rivera at gmail dot com”, not digits and @ signs, so regex and NER tools miss them. VoiceSafeKit reads spoken numbers, emails and dates as written form, finds cards, one-time codes, IDs and credentials, then redacts the original transcript in your browser before an LLM sees it.

Simple workflow

Capture. Check. Protect.

01

Capture

Record your voice, upload a transcript or paste text directly. Conversation mode lets you chain turns and track risk across the session.

02

Check

Detect PII, credentials, one-time codes, SSNs, IBANs, emotional distress and coercion signals, including details spoken as words. Every finding shows a confidence score and inline highlight.

03

Protect

Get a redacted transcript, a detailed redaction map and simple guidance. Export the full result as encrypted JSON when you need to save it.
